Medical Physics Support, from Planning to Practice

A flexible scope of services tailored to your institution's facilities, license, clinical workflow, and qualified personnel. Services are subject to applicable state and federal requirements and individual professional authorization.

01

Program Readiness Assessment

Review existing nuclear medicine infrastructure, treatment spaces, patient flow, instrumentation, staffing interfaces, and implementation gaps.

02

Radiation Safety & Licensing Support

Assist with radiation safety planning, radioactive material license application or amendment documentation, shielding and exposure assessments, and coordination with the institution's RSO.

03

Facility & Workflow Design

Support patient treatment room preparation, radioactive material handling pathways, contamination controls, waste management, and patient release workflow planning.

04

Surveys, Instrumentation & QA

Develop survey methods and documentation, evaluate appropriate detection instrumentation, and support acceptance and routine quality assurance procedures.

05

Procedures & Staff Training

Help draft institution-specific SOPs, checklists, incident response guidance, and radiation safety training materials for multidisciplinary teams.

06

Ongoing Physics Consultation

Provide project-based or recurring support for operational reviews, radiation protection optimization, audits, and physics-related troubleshooting.

Important: Medical physics consulting supports—but does not replace—the responsibilities of the authorized user physician, radiation safety officer, authorized medical physicist where required, licensee, and other qualified clinical personnel.
